One report, a complete response

Built around the workflow, not just a form

Every stage below is implemented against the same SQLite database. New reports immediately affect the dashboard, map and hotspot analysis.

1

Citizen reports

Title, description, category, location and an optional photo. No account or personal details required.

2

AI analyses

Local NLP predicts the category, writes an extractive summary and computes an explainable 0–100 priority score.

3

System connects

TF-IDF text similarity plus geographic distance suggests possible duplicates and reveals clustered hotspots.

4

Officer acts

Review the evidence, assign a department, change the status and leave an internal or public note.

5

Citizen tracks

The tracking page reads persisted status history — never just a frontend state — so progress is visible.
